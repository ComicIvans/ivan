::event-about
The National Meeting of Mathematics Students (ENEM) is organised annually by the National Association of Mathematics Students (ANEM). Since its inception in Granada in 2000, ENEM has served as the annual meeting point for hundreds of students, offering a unique opportunity to network and share knowledge and experiences. Each year, it continues to grow and reach a wider audience, establishing itself as the flagship annual event within the student community of these fields.

This conference is much more than an academic gathering; it is a platform for fostering synergies, inspiration, and motivation. Participants have the opportunity to delve into various areas of mathematics, statistics, and data science, while also promoting the importance of these disciplines in today’s society.

Under the motto “From Granada to Spain and back to Granada”, the 26th ENEM marked the 25th anniversary of the event and was held in its birthplace from 22 to 27 July 2025. As the original host city and the heart of ENEM, Granada offers a comprehensive and enriching environment in terms of mathematics, culture, society, and industry.

**Activities**

The 26th ENEM is an event featuring a wide range of enriching activities, including several initiatives aimed at bringing the professional world closer to students, such as the Business and Entrepreneurship Forum and the PechaKuchas.

- **Plenary lectures**: The core of the event consists of popular-science plenary lectures aimed at all attendees. These talks range from 45 to 75 minutes and include a Q&A session.
- **Round tables**: Discussion spaces where experts share their perspectives on various topics of interest that generate debate or concern within the mathematical community.
- **Workshops**: Smaller sessions offering a close, relaxed, and hands-on experience.
- **Microtalks**: Short presentations that allow attendees to present ideas, projects, or results in a concise and dynamic format.
- **Business and Entrepreneurship Forum**: An excellent opportunity to establish contact with organisations related to mathematics and affiliated sectors.
- **PechaKuchas**: A dynamic presentation format consisting of 20 slides shown for 20 seconds each.
::

::event-participation
I was part of the Organising Committee of the 26th ENEM, serving as **Treasurer and Secretary**, and participating in the organisation of the event from February 2024, when our bid was selected as the host venue.

My main responsibilities included managing the event budget, securing sponsors, coordinating with the entities participating in the Business and Entrepreneurship Forum, as well as planning and overseeing the overall logistics (accommodation, catering, venues, among other aspects).

Thanks to the coordinated work and strong commitment of the organising team, we contributed to the delivery of what was regarded as the best edition of the event to date, achieving record participation figures, a wide variety of activities, and a high level of overall attendee satisfaction.
::
